Virgo Sudoku — Privacy Policy

Effective 30 August 2026 · MissileToad Productions

Virgo Sudoku is a Sudoku game for Android, published by MissileToad Productions. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device. It applies to the app only, not to any website or other service.

Summary

The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. It does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location or files, and nothing you create in the app leaves your device. The only information that leaves your device is handled by Google's advertising SDK, described below.

Information stored on your device

Your puzzles in progress, completion statistics and settings are written to the app's own private storage on your device. They are not transmitted anywhere, are not readable by other apps, and are deleted when you uninstall the app. There is no cloud backup and no way for us to see them.

Advertising

The app displays a single banner advertisement supplied by Google AdMob. To select and measure those ads, Google's advertising SDK may collect device information and your Android advertising ID, and may use them to personalise ads. This processing takes place inside Google's SDK; the app itself neither reads nor stores that information, and we receive no personal data from it.

How Google uses this data is described in Google's policy for partner sites and apps. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and limit ad personalisation, in your device settings under Google → Ads.

Where consent is required by law — in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland — the app shows Google's consent message before any personalised advertising request is made. You can revisit that choice at any time from Settings → Privacy → Ad Privacy Choices in the app.

Background images

The app downloads public-domain photography from NASA's Image and Video Library to use as backgrounds, and stores the images on your device. These requests are ordinary image downloads; no account or identifier of yours is sent with them.

You may also choose a folder of your own pictures to use instead. If you turn that option on, Android asks you to grant access to a single folder that you select. The app reads those images only to display them as backgrounds. They are never copied, uploaded or shared, and no information about them leaves your device. You can withdraw the permission by turning the option off in the app, or in Android's settings for the app.

Permissions

  • Internet access — to download background imagery and to load advertisements.
  • Vibration — for the optional touch feedback that can be switched off in Settings.
  • Folder access (optional) — granted by you, only if you enable your own pictures as backgrounds, and only for the folder you choose.

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or general storage permissions.

Children

Virgo Sudoku is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising served in the app is limited to a general-audience content rating.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of information changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised.
